About Car Kenya

Driving Your Next Journey

Car Kenya is built around making the vehicle discovery and sourcing experience simpler, clearer and more convenient.

Who We Are

Your Vehicle.
Your Choice.

Car Kenya is a premium automotive platform focused on helping customers discover and source vehicles for the Kenyan market.

Whether you are looking for a locally available vehicle or considering a foreign-used vehicle, our goal is to provide useful information that makes the buying process easier to understand.

From vehicle selection and sourcing to inspection and import assistance, Car Kenya brings different parts of the vehicle journey together in one place.

Our Mission

Simplify Vehicle Discovery

Our mission is to make it easier for customers to discover suitable vehicles and understand the steps involved in purchasing or sourcing them.

Our Vision

A Better Automotive Experience
